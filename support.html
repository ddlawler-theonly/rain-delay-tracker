How it works

  • Add a job with its address (or drop a pin / draw the site area on the map), NTP / start date, completion date, and rain rule (default: 0.50 in or more, plus the next day if it's wet out).
  • Coverage: all 50 U.S. states and Washington, DC. The app picks the nearest airport (ASOS) weather station that reports rainfall and fills in every day from the start date through yesterday. Tap Refresh any time.
  • Days at or over the threshold are flagged as rain days. The next day is flagged "possible wet-out" for you to confirm or reject. Log crew status (worked / partial / rained out) and notes.
  • Export the weather-day log as CSV, Excel, or PDF for a time-extension request, or back up all data as JSON.

Data sources

Daily observations: Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS daily summaries (these can differ slightly from the official NWS Climatological Report). Forecast: National Weather Service. Normals: NOAA NCEI 1991-2020. Missing data is shown as missing, never estimated.
