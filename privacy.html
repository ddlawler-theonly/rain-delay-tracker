Rain Out - Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · Publisher: BlueWorks Field Pro (Derek Lawler)

Summary

Rain Out does not collect, sell, or share personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Your jobs, notes, and logs are stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

Job names, numbers, addresses, coordinates, dates, rain rules, daily weather records, crew status, and notes are saved in the app's local storage on your device (and, in the iOS app, in a backup file in the app's Documents folder that you can see in the Files app). We have no server and cannot see this data. Deleting the app deletes it. You can export it at any time (CSV, Excel, PDF, JSON backup); you choose where exports go.

Network requests the app makes

To do its job the app contacts these public services directly from your device. They receive your device's IP address and the request contents, under their own privacy policies:

  • Iowa Environmental Mesonet (mesonet.agron.iastate.edu, Iowa State University) - daily weather observations for the weather station ID and date range of each job. No personal data is sent.
  • U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) - today's forecast for a job's latitude/longitude.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) - only when you tap "Find lat/lon" or search the map: the address or place text you typed.
  • Map tiles - only when you open a map: OpenStreetMap tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org) and, if you choose Satellite, the U.S. Geological Survey's National Map (basemap.nationalmap.gov) receive the map area being viewed.

The app does not use your device's GPS/location services.

In-app purchases (iPhone app)

Rain Out Pro subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account, or payment details. The app checks whether your subscription is active on the device using Apple's StoreKit; it does not send purchase information to us or to any other server. Apple handles purchase data under Apple's privacy policy. Manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Notifications

The iOS app can show a local notification when a new rain day is flagged. Notifications are generated on the device; no push service is used.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.
